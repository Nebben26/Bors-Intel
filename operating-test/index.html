U.S. Media Entry Blueprint

Know what to build before you fund the media company.

Before a global organization commits to a launch, Bors Intel turns the commercial objective into a specific American media asset. The blueprint defines the audience, brand, programming, distribution, subscriber system, budget, measurement, and operating model.

$20K–$50K Typical scope · 3–6 weeks · fixed deliverables

Final scope depends on market breadth, research depth, and whether prototype creative is included.

Discuss a blueprint ↗

Six decision-ready deliverables

  1. 01Commercial objectiveThe market problem, strategic value, and outcome the asset must create.
  2. 02American audience mapThe exact people to reach, why they matter, and how they currently behave.
  3. 03Brand and positioning thesisA credible media identity built for the audience, not around corporate promotion.
  4. 04Programming slateFlagship formats, recurring franchises, short-form system, and editorial cadence.
  5. 05Distribution systemOrganic channels, subscriber capture, lifecycle, partnerships, and measurement.
  6. 0612-month operating modelPilot scope, team, budget, workflow, KPIs, risks, and launch recommendation.

The blueprint is the first commercial step for organizations considering a funded U.S. media asset. It can stand alone as a decision document or become the foundation for a 90–120 day launch pilot, ongoing network operation, or a longer-term partnership structure.
