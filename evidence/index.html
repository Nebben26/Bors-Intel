The five featured Reel counts come from each video’s public Facebook page: 550K, 196K, 115K, 83K, and 76K views when checked July 16, 2026. Email totals come from first-party Beehiiv dashboard captures for five campaigns sent April through July 2026.

Whop publicly displays $210K+ earned on Ben’s creator profile and a 4.5/5 rating from eight ratings on The Fifth Signal listing. The figure is presented as a platform-listed creator total and is not attributed to any single campaign. Independent Bors coverage was published by The Daily Scrum News in June 2023.

Revenue figures combine two non-overlapping platform records. A first-party Bankful report supplied by Ben shows $570,153.25 in gross sales, $1,497.50 in refunds, and $568,655.75 in total sales after refunds. Whop separately lists more than $210,000 earned on Ben’s creator profile. Together, the records exceed $778,000; the studio uses a conservative shorthand. These are platform-reported sales and earnings, not profit, ARR, or revenue from one campaign. The 0.26% refund rate applies only to Bankful gross sales. The peak-month result comes from a separate first-party processor report available for review during commercial diligence.

Ben designed, built, deployed, and maintains both The Fifth Signal and BorsIntel.com. The work includes site strategy, information architecture, conversion copy, responsive front-end development, integrations, quality assurance, GitHub version control, Netlify deployment, and ongoing iteration and release workflows.

AI workflow claims refer to private systems Ben personally designed and uses for research, content operations, analysis, and decision support. The systems include documented context, source controls, prompt sequences, brand and claim guardrails, quality checks, escalation rules, and human approval. “Team training” describes Ben’s ability to package and teach these workflows; it is not a claim that he trained or fine-tuned an AI model.
