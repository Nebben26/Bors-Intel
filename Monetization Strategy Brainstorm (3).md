# Hard corrections to the previous planning

## 1. Co-registration revenue is not MRR

Per-lead revenue can fund the company and may be substantial, but variable lead sales do not count toward $55,000 MRR unless buyers sign recurring monthly minimums.

The system may contain ten buyer slots. Until there are contracts, caps, prices, consent requirements, acceptance rules, prepayments and successful delivery, those slots have no monetary value.

# Non-product monetization: simple unit economics

These exclude the **$27 Archive, $45/month Power Files Weekly, and $97/month Signal Feed**.

The core formula for every lead-based engine is:

```
Collected value per signup
=
eligibility rate
× accepted-event rate
× payout
× collection rate
− direct variable cost
```

The numbers below are **underwriting assumptions to test**, not guaranteed market prices. Public benchmarks exist for paid recommendations, adviser referrals, affiliate commissions, and sponsorships; direct co-reg, SMS, enrichment, and audience deals are usually privately negotiated.
